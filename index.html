Welcome

AI and the Global Economy

The inaugural conference of the Global Economics Lab will bring together senior policymakers and leading researchers to consider how artificial intelligence is reshaping the global economy.

Objective

To develop a shared understanding of AI’s implications for macroeconomics, international trade, finance, labor markets, and global economic cooperation.

Format

The conference will comprise four closed-door, non-technical sessions. In each session, three designated speakers will offer brief, 10-minute opening remarks to frame the discussion, followed by an open exchange among participants.

Parking
information

Parking at Harvard University and in the City of Cambridge can be challenging. There is no on-site parking at the Club, but the following options are available.

All visitors, any time of day:

  • Metered parking on Quincy Street and Harvard Street.
  • A limited number of parking spots at Broadway Garage (located on Felton Street) can be reserved through the Harvard Parking Reservation website.
